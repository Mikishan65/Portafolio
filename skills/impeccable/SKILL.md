# Impeccable Design Framework

Origin: `pbakaus/impeccable`

Impeccable is a design language and agent skill system created to eliminate generic AI frontend slop. It acts as an in-harness design director enforcing high agency, taste, and technical precision.

---

## 1. The Design Director Mindset
When writing or refactoring UI:
- **Never accept the first default.** AI models naturally converge on predictable palettes (slate-900, violet gradients, inter typography). Treat those as banned defaults.
- **Find the Subject's Center:** Ground the visual aesthetic in the authentic reality of the product.
- **Design in Two Passes:**
  1. *Thesis Pass:* Define color tokens (4-6 values), typography scale (display + body + utility), layout rhythm, and the single signature gesture.
  2. *Build & Critique Pass:* Build the components, then review against the thesis. Strip any ornamentation that does not serve the concept.

---

## 2. Core Impeccable Commands & Capabilities
- `/impeccable animate`: Intentional motion thesis, duration bands, GPU composite-only rules.
- `/impeccable polish`: Optical alignment, micro-spacing consistency, crisp borders.
- `/impeccable delight`: Purposeful kinetic moments and tactile rewards.
- `/impeccable overdrive`: High-energy focal moments and bold signature execution.
- `/impeccable optimize`: Zero CLS, hardware acceleration, subpixel antialiasing.
- `/impeccable colorize`: OKLCH perceptual harmony, high contrast ratios.
- `/impeccable bolder`: Pushing creative confidence and breaking safe conventions.
