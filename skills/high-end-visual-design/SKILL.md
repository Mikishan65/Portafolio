# High-End Visual Design Skill

Origin: `Leonxlnx/taste-skill`

High-end digital design conveys authority, intentionality, and restraint. It avoids decorative clutter in favor of typographic precision, rich textures, and spatial tension.

---

## 1. Principles of Luxury & High-Craft Web UI
- **Restraint Over Clutter:** Confidence is shown by what is omitted. If an element does not inform, orient, or evoke emotion, remove it.
- **Asymmetric Tension:** Avoid centered, predictable 3-column rows. Create visual interest with weighted columns (e.g., 60/40 splits) and deliberate negative space.
- **Micro-Tension in Typography:** Pair an expressive, authoritative display face (tight negative letter-spacing) with a neutral, generous body font.

---

## 2. Color Restraint
- **The 80/15/5 Rule:**
  - 80% Canvas / Neutral background.
  - 15% Structural contrast (text, hairline borders).
  - 5% Single sharp accent color used for focal moments and interactions.
- Avoid multi-stop bright gradients. Embrace solid tones, rich dark pigments, or warm stone canvas colors.

---

## 3. Surface & Elevation
- Ditch fuzzy, ambient drop shadows (`box-shadow: 0 20px 40px rgba(...)`).
- Build depth with:
  - 1px hairline technical borders (`rgba(0,0,0,0.08)` or `rgba(255,255,255,0.1)`).
  - Subtle tonal shifts between background and elevated panels.
  - Crisp, slight offsets (e.g. 1px–2px directional borders).
