# Impeccable Bolder Skill

Origin: `pbakaus/impeccable`

A timid interface is forgotten immediately. Bolder pushes the AI agent beyond "safe" middle-of-the-road outputs into memorable, distinct design territory.

---

## 1. The Single Bold Risk Rule
In every project or page, take **one real aesthetic risk** that can be defended by product truth:
- A non-traditional color pairing that commands attention.
- A distinctive typography treatment (e.g., condensed heavy display font, unexpected serif/mono juxtaposition).
- An asymmetric, kinetic composition that breaks the standard rectangular container grid.
- An interactive spatial gesture (e.g. ambient orbit, magnetic physics).

---

## 2. Quiet Discipline Everywhere Else
Taking a risk requires absolute discipline around it:
- Let the bold element be the sole hero.
- Strip away secondary decorations, extra borders, and superfluous badges.
- Ensure the core mechanics (navigation, reading, accessibility, mobile layout) remain rock-solid and intuitive.
