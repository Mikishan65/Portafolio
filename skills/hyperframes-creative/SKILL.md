# Hyperframes Creative Direction Skill

Origin: `heygen-com/hyperframes`

Creative guidelines for high-impact programmatic video design across social feeds, product showcases, and broadcast reels.

---

## 1. Aspect Ratio Staging
- **16:9 Widescreen (1920x1080):** Desktop landscapes, YouTube presentations, and corporate overviews. Keep safe title margins within 80% of outer edges.
- **9:16 Vertical (1080x1920):** TikTok, Reels, Shorts. Reserve the bottom 25% (UI overlay zone) and top 15% (header zone) free of essential text.
- **1:1 Square (1080x1080):** Feed carousels and multi-platform promotional stings.

---

## 2. Broadcast Typography Rules
- **Minimum Font Sizes:** Never render body text smaller than `28px` in 1080p compositions; mobile viewers cannot read micro-typography in video.
- **High Contrast Backgrounds:** Place text on deep contrast backdrops or high-contrast solid plates; never lay thin text over moving, complex video backgrounds without a scrim.
