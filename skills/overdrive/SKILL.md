# Impeccable Overdrive Skill

Origin: `pbakaus/impeccable`

Overdrive is deployed when an experience needs to command total attention, break conventions, and leave a permanent visual impression.

---

## 1. When to Engage Overdrive
- Portfolios, product drops, keynote teasers, and agency landing pages.
- When standard, subdued corporate designs fail to convey brand velocity or breakthrough innovation.

---

## 2. Overdrive Execution Rules
- **The Giant Thesis:** Display type that spans fluidly across the viewport (`clamp(3rem, 10vw, 8rem)`), tracked tight with high-impact font silhouettes (`Anybody`, `Clash Display`, `Syne`).
- **Kinetic Atmosphere:** Ambient background motion (orbiting vectors, GLSL mesh distortions, subtle grain overlays) that runs smoothly at 60fps on GPU composited layers.
- **Maximalist Accents, Minimalist Core:** High energy in the hero and visual signatures, paired with surgical, ultra-clean typography in reading and content zones.
